Write an essay of at least 1,200 words on how household refrigeration changed everyday life in the twentieth century.

Requirements:
1. Use at least six paragraphs: shopping and markets, cooking at home, family meals, health and food safety, the shape of cities and suburbs, and what was lost along the way.
2. Give every paragraph concrete scenes and examples rather than general statements.
3. Write continuous prose: no headings, no bullet points, no tables.
4. Keep writing until the essay is complete; do not stop early and do not end with a summary.
